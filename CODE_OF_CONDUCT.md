# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want PROCESIO CLI + Agent to be a project anyone can contribute to. Everyone taking part -
maintainers, contributors, people opening an issue - is expected to treat others with
respect, regardless of who they are or where they come from.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about the problem, not the person.
- Say what you mean plainly, and accept plain feedback in return.
- Keep discussion on the technical question. Review the change, not its author.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody is keeping score.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of a
discussion, and publishing anyone's private information without their consent.

## Reporting

Report a problem to Ringhel TEAM SRL (PROCESIO) at the contact address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.
We will respond, and we will say what we decided.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
