# Freshness policy

## Volatile claims

Verify these before answering:

- pricing, packaging, limits, trials, and contract terms;
- release status, roadmap, supported actions/connectors/languages/databases;
- hosting locations and deployment requirements;
- certifications, compliance modes, retention, and security features;
- company, partner, customer, and competitor claims;
- performance benchmarks and cost-reduction percentages.

Prefer, in order:

1. Current first-party product documentation or contract material the user supplies.
2. Live read-only CLI evidence from the target installation.
3. Current first-party website/release notes.
4. A clearly labeled historical repository reference.

Record `source`, `verified_on`, `scope`, and `confidence`. When sources conflict, state the conflict instead of choosing the more favorable claim.

## Stable guidance

The evaluation method, units, proof standards, and distinction between shipped/custom/external/roadmap are durable. Keep those in SKILL.md. Keep dated facts in references or generated evidence.
